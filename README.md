## What is Naffo?

**Naffo Technologies** builds modern business software for businesses that have outgrown spreadsheets, disconnected tools, and traditional ERP workflows.

Our platform, **[naffo.tech](https://naffo.tech/)**, brings together:

> **ERP + AI + Automation + Business Intelligence**

From accounting and GST to inventory, manufacturing, dairy operations, retail, and business analytics — Naffo connects everyday business activity with the information needed to make better decisions.

## AI + ERP

### Your ERP. Your Data. Your AI.

Naffo is designed to bring AI closer to day-to-day business operations.

With the **Naffo MCP endpoint**, compatible AI clients can interact with supported ERP workflows according to configured access and permissions.

```text
https://naffo.tech/api/mcp
```

### Connect your business with AI

**Claude** • **ChatGPT** • **Gemini** • **MCP-compatible clients**

Examples of natural-language business requests:

```text
"Show today's sales."

"Which customers have outstanding payments?"

"Check current stock for Butter 500g."

"Show product-wise sales and margin."

"Which items are nearing expiry?"
```
